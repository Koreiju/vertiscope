## The question, as put

> An open page in the 3D view currently draws every chunk ever stored for that
> URL. On the Julia forum's /latest that's ~1,000 topics seen across visits and
> scrolls -- most no longer on the page -- and it's now the main render cost
> (2,467 nodes, 1,476 textures; diffs take 157-508 ms to reach the scene). What
> should an open page draw?

Options offered: *What's on the page now* (recommended by me), *Everything
stored*, *Recent history*.

## The answer (the user's choice)

**Everything stored.** With it the note offered on that option: "Keep drawing
the page's whole history; I'd attack the cost with instancing and texture
atlases instead."

## What it constrains

[[L1.pg-062.render]] may not meet its frame budget by drawing less. Culling
stored chunks, trimming history or drawing only the live page are all ruled
out; the cost has to come out of HOW the scene is drawn -- one draw call per
node and per link is what has to go.

## How the constraint could be misread

"Everything stored" is about what is DRAWN for an open page. It does not say a
closed page must draw its chunks, and it does not rule out level of detail
(a far node drawn as a point rather than a picture) -- that is still drawing it.
