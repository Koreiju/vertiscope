- `vertiscope/obsidian/refitscope.py` -- no torch, no store:
  - `RefitCost` -- `observe(rows, ms)`, `predict(rows)`: a least-squares line
    through the measured points once two distinct row counts exist, the
    2026-09-28 curve before that (`seeded` says which).
  - `choose_scope(page_rows, chunk_rows, cost, budget_ms, engine, centroid)`
    -> `{scope, why, rows, predicted_ms, budget_ms}`.
- `PageProjector`:
  - `SCOPE_BUDGET_MS = 5000`; `scope`, `scope_record`, `refit_cost`,
    `trained_chunks`.
  - `fit`: decide; in chunk scope the first fit trains on
    `vstack(page centroids, unit chunk vectors)` and the online refit adds
    untrained chunk rows; every fit/update observes its cost.
  - `_online_worker`: in chunk scope, the untrained chunks of the pages it
    trains join the update.
  - `status()` and every fit record carry `scope` and the cost model.
- Tests: `tests/test_refit_scope.py` -- the decision with planted budgets, the
  cost model, and a chunk-scope fit whose model was trained on the chunks.
