## What was measured, and against what

In the running app on the working store (`build/corpus123`), the reader's own
pane, three windows in a row on the same graph: batch, the per-node objects
planted back (`setPerNode(true)`), batch again. Each window 6 s. Picking was
tested through 3d-force-graph's own hover path: a `pointermove` at a node's
projected screen position, then the tooltip the graph writes a frame later
compared with that node's label.

| 1,839 nodes, 1,685 links | draw calls / frame | render CPU p50 | p95 | textures | geometries | picked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| batch | **2** | **1.2 ms** | 1.9 ms | 1 | 4 | 7 of 8 |
| per-node (planted) | 3,524 | 31.1 ms | 449 ms | 1,805 | 6 | 7 of 8 |
| batch again | 2 | 1.1 ms | 5.3 ms | 1 | 4 | -- |

An earlier window at 2,467 nodes: 2 draw calls against 4,780; textures 1
against 2,283; picking 6 of 8 in both arms.

The one miss in both arms (and both in the earlier one) is the same node each
time: a books.toscrape page whose label the tooltip gave to a different page
node in front of it. It is occlusion, not the proxy: the per-node arm misses it
identically.

Batch instances drawn = nodes in the graph (1,839 = 1,839 opaque + 0
translucent; nothing streaming at the time), which is demand 2 (nothing
stored dropped). 745 pictures went into the atlas; `atlasFull` 0.

## In the Verifier, on a fresh store (check G-107, PASS)

The check lands the Julia forum's /latest (the page the render cost was
measured on) and scrolls it, then runs three arms on that one graph:

| forum, 198 nodes, 184 links | draw calls | render CPU p50 | textures | picks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| batch | 2 | 0.2 ms | 1 | 10 / 10 |
| per-node (planted) | 381 | 1.5 ms | 189 | 10 / 10 |
| no-pick proxies (planted) | 2 | 0.3 ms | 1 | 0 / 10 |

It failed twice on the way, both usefully. The first run found picking at
8 of 10 against the sprites' 10: proxies sized to the collider's
half-DIAGONAL reached past a picture's drawn square into its neighbours;
sized to the drawn half-extent, 10 of 10. The second failed a 500-node floor
the fresh store could not reach (198); the bound is now shown the way it is
meant -- the planted arm pays at least one call per node on the same graph.

In the stress gesture (30 s rapid scroll on the forum): 3 draw calls with
584 translucent streaming nodes, 0.4 ms render CPU p50, frame gaps p95
8-12 ms, every pushed drain applied in 3D.

## What does NOT count as evidence here

- **Frame gaps.** The window was in the background and Chromium throttled
  its animation frames to ~1 Hz: 1,000 ms gaps in both arms. They are
  reported and never judged. The render call's CPU time is the measure of
  what the draw calls cost.
- **GPU time.** Not measured. `renderer.render` CPU time covers command
  submission, which is where per-object draw calls cost on ANGLE/Direct3D,
  but a fill-rate-bound scene would hide from it.

## How this evidence could be wrong

- Draw calls are counted with `info.autoReset = false` and reset from the
  pane's own rAF tick. If 3d-force-graph rendered twice in one tick, a tick
  would count both, and `calls / frames` divides by the frame counter
  to stay per-frame. A frame counted by nobody (no rAF tick between two
  renders) folds into the next.
- Picking was tested on the 8-10 NEAREST projected nodes. Nodes deep in a
  dense cloud were not asked. The no-pick planted arm shows that the test
  depends on the proxies, not that every node is reachable.
