## Verbatim

> I'd also like you to include the updates on the 3D graph viewer as well,
> ensure that the diff updates are sent from the browser/electron scanner and
> received on the other end within the same diff update latency and
> proportion in rendering. I think there might be some blockage on the 3D side
> where we stop seeing any updates from the scanner side at all.

## Reading

**This is my reading, not the requirement.** The 3D view is a receiver of the same diff stream the overlay draws, and it is owed the same latency and the same PROPORTION: every chunk the scanner emits as new should appear in 3D, as fast as it appears outlined. The suspicion is a blockage on the 3D side where updates stop arriving at all.

Projected into [[L1.pg-062.stream-3d]].

**How this reading could be wrong.** "Proportion in rendering" might mean visual scale (node sizes proportional to the diff), not the count ratio I read.
