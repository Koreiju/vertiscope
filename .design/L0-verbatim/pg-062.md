## Reading

**This is my reading, not the requirement.** Beyond merging the loops, the sentence dictates the ORDER of the layout pipeline and what each step owes the next: (1) the in-page recursive chunking turns mutations into a deduplicated chunk diff in O(delta) and outlines it in milliseconds; (2) the diff streams straight to the 3D view, force-directed and relaxed, BEFORE the store; (3) the store saves after the GUI has drawn, with vectorization beside it; (4) a node the store confirms saved collapses into its page root; (5) its vector is placed through the current UMAP fit, replacing the force position, with collision (an integer safety factor) as the only force after; (6) a root click toggles the whole tree; (7) UMAP coordinates persist, force never, and a refit re-projects every stored chunk. It also binds the loop to judge by what a human would say about the picture.

**How this reading could be wrong.** I read steps (1)-(3) as a latency ORDER (draw before persist); the sentence also allows reading "before even saving to DB if possible/necessary" as optional where saving first is cheaper. And "subchunk centroid" may mean something finer than a chunk's own vector.
